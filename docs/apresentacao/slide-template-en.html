Research Program

How Generative AI Impacts Cognitive Development of Children (2026-2030)

UFRN/CERES · PPGED · ICe
Advisor: Profa. Dra. Ângela M. C. Naschold
CAPES Scholarship Holder

Context and Justification

  • Explosive growth of generative AI in schools (ChatGPT, Khanmigo, Claude)
  • Brazilian National AI Plan 2024-2028 includes public school integration
  • BNCC (National Curriculum) includes computational thinking
  • Gap: little is known about how young children perceive and use AI
  • Critical population: 2nd grade (7-8 years) during literacy acquisition

Research Question

P01 (qualitative):

"How do 2nd-grade children interpret, experience, and trust the AI tutor Khanmigo?"

Program (5 studies):

  • P01: Reflexive Thematic Analysis (qualitative)
  • P02: RCT 2×4 (gamification × EF)
  • P03: Quasi-experimental (EEG N170/P300)
  • P04: Cross-sectional (SEM, N=300-500)
  • P05: 5-year Longitudinal Cohort (N=200)

Method — P01 Pilot

Participants

  • 3 children (7-8 years)
  • 2nd grade, public school
  • Natal/RN, Brazil

Instruments

  • Semi-structured interviews
  • Think-aloud
  • Usage diaries (15-17 days)
  • Parent + teacher questionnaires

Analysis

  • Reflexive Thematic Analysis
  • Braun & Clarke (2022)
  • Complementary statistical validation

Ethics

  • IRB/UFRN approved
  • Parental consent + child assent
  • LGPD compliant

5 Emergent Themes

1. Conditional Anthropomorphization

Human pronouns + recognition of limits. "Khanmigo is a robot that helps"

2. Early Error Detection

Children identify and correct the tutor. "I counted on my fingers and showed him"

3. Calibrated Trust

Active verification. "I trust but I verify" (Pedro)

4. Systematic Comparison

Evaluation against humans (teacher, family, friends)

5. Contextual Preference

Situational choice. "For math I use [Khanmigo], for reading I prefer my grandma"

Statistical validation

All 5 themes appeared in 100% of children. Pilot statistical analysis available.

Statistical Validation of 5 Themes

Tests performed (N=3, pilot)

  • Math > reading confidence: 3/3 directions, consistent (p=0.125, NS due to small N)
  • Use × error detection: ρ = 0.50, 95% CI bootstrap [-1, 1]
  • Usage temporal trend: Mann-Kendall Z = 0, p = 1.0 (stable)
  • Activity distribution: χ² = 8.5, p < 0.05 (non-uniform, math 57%)

Implication

✅ All 5 themes are quantitatively supported even with N=3

Exploratory analysis. Replicate with N=12-15 for thematic saturation + adequate statistical power.

Implications

Theoretical

  • MToM is multi-level (not dichotomous)
  • MToM is contextual
  • MToM is educable (parents, school)

Practical

  • Schools: AI complements, doesn't replace teacher
  • Parents: discuss how to trust AI
  • Designers: make errors visible (calibration)
  • Policy: critical digital literacy in curriculum
