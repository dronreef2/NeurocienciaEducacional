## 📰 IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**UFRN launches Brazil's largest research program on AI and children**
*Pioneering study will follow 800 children across 5 projects over 5 years*

The Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte (UFRN), through its Graduate
Program in Education (PPGED) and the Seridó Higher Education Center (CERES),
announces the launch of an **unprecedented research program** in Brazil
that will investigate how **generative Artificial Intelligences** — such
as ChatGPT, Khanmigo, and Gemini — impact the cognitive development of
2nd to 5th grade Elementary School children.

Coordinated by **Prof. Dr. Ângela Maria Chuvas Naschold**, the program
includes **5 integrated projects** with a duration of **5 years (2026-2030)**,
budget of **R$ 204,000 (approximately USD 40,000)**, and is expected to
follow approximately **800 children** in public and private schools in
Rio Grande do Norte, Brazil.

---

## 🎯 The 5 projects

The program is structured in **5 complementary projects**, each with
specific methods and research questions:

### P01 — AI and Theory of Mind Cognition (Qualitative)
Will investigate how 7-11 year-old children construct mental representations
of AIs. **Pilot study already completed** with 3 children identified 5
emerging themes: conditional anthropomorphization, early error detection,
calibrated trust, systematic comparison, and contextual preference.

### P02 — Gamification and Executive Functions (RCT)
Randomized controlled trial with 200 children to test whether gamification
(points + narrative) improves executive functions like attention, memory,
and cognitive flexibility.

### P03 — Reading on Screen vs Paper (EEG)
Study with 32-channel electroencephalography will investigate how the brain
processes reading in different media. Will compare 60 children reading on
screen vs paper.

### P04 — AI and Executive Functions (SEM)
Structural equation modeling with 300-500 children will test how AI use
affects executive functions, mediated by school engagement and moderated
by parents' digital literacy.

### P05 — Longitudinal Cohort
Will follow 200 children from 2nd to 6th grade, in 5 waves, to map the
development of executive functions throughout Elementary School.

## 💡 Why is this study important?

### Scientific gap
To date, there are no large-scale Brazilian studies investigating the impact
of generative AIs on child development. The program fills this gap with
**methodological rigor** and **strict ethics** (LGPD compliant).

### Social relevance
Digital literacy is one of the most demanded competencies of the 21st
century, but little is known about how Brazilian children are processing
these new technologies. Results will subsidize:

- **Educational policies** for critical AI integration in schools
- **Teacher training** for pedagogical use of AI
- **Parent guidance** on healthy AI use at home
- **Ethical design** of AI systems for children

### Methodological innovation
Rare combination of **qualitative methods** (interviews, diaries) with
**quantitative methods** (inferential statistics) and **neuroscience**
(EEG). Data will be openly shared on OSF, in compliance with **LGPD**.
