🧠 Study on AI Tutors for Children

Educational Neuroscience Research Program — UFRN/CERES

Academic Research IRB Approved Voluntary

Invitation to Participate

Are you a parent/guardian of a child in 2nd to 5th grade at a public or private school in Natal/RN, Brazil? We invite you and your child to participate in research investigating how children use and perceive AI tutors (such as Khanmigo).

📋 What is the study?

This study is part of a 5-year research program at UFRN/CERES, coordinated by Profa. Dra. Ângela Maria Chuvas Naschold. We investigate how different AI-based educational technologies impact children's cognitive development.

👶 Who can participate?

Inclusion Criteria

  • Children enrolled in 2nd to 5th grade of Elementary School
  • Age 7-11 years
  • Access to internet-connected device (computer, tablet, or phone)
  • Parental consent + child assent

Exclusion Criteria

  • Diagnosis of cognitive disability
  • Continuous use of psychoactive medication
  • History of epilepsy (only for EEG sub-study)

🔬 What does participation involve?

1

Regular Use

Your child will use Khanmigo for 8 weeks, 3x/week, 20-30 minutes per session.

2

Interview

A 30-45 minute interview with your child about their experience with the tutor.

3

Questionnaires

You and the teacher will fill out online questionnaires (15 min each).

4

Usage Diary

For 2 weeks, you record Khanmigo usage time (5 min/day).

🔒 Privacy and Data Protection (LGPD)

✅ Your data is protected by LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law)
• All information is anonymous (children identified as C01, C02, ...)
• Data stored on secure server (UFRN)
• You can withdraw consent at any time, without penalty
• Results will be published only in aggregate form
• You will receive feedback on results at the end
